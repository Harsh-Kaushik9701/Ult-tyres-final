# Ultimate Tyres logo directions

Early vector directions based on the brief’s existing UT mark and high-visibility red, charcoal and white palette. These are review concepts, not approved final artwork.

- Option A: UT block mark — bold initials with the full wordmark, optimized for workshop signage and small digital placements. See public/brand-options/option-a.svg.
- Option B: Roadline wordmark — a cleaner, lighter lockup with a forward motion cue. See public/brand-options/option-b.svg.
- Option C: Monogram — a compact U form with the campaign line. See public/brand-options/option-c.svg.

The site currently uses a temporary UT monogram. Final logo selection, trademark review and production artwork remain to be approved.
